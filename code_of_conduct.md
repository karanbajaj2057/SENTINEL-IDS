# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical issues and evidence.
- Welcome questions from different experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior.
- Protect privacy and security in examples and shared data.

Unacceptable behavior may result in moderation or removal from project spaces.
